The first IDEAS All-Hands Meeting brings the whole IDEAS team — Argonne and Partners — to Argonne National Laboratory for two days of research talks, use-case working sessions, software demos and planning.

Tuesday, February 2 runs a full day. Wednesday, February 3 is a half day, to allow for travel.

Attendance is by invitation only. All IDEAS participants should plan to attend; the RSVP form, agenda, room and travel details will be announced on this page as they are confirmed.
