# IDEAS Project Website

The public website for IDEAS, one research thrust of the Argonne Base program. It presents the project's research, science use cases, team, publications, and events to an external audience.

## Language

### Program structure

**IDEAS**:
Intelligent Data Exploration Assistant for Science (canonical expansion) — the visualization thrust (Thrust 2) of Argonne Base, led by Victor Mateevitsi. The sole subject of this site.
_Avoid_: the project (ambiguous with Argonne Base), Viz thrust, "Intelligent Data Exploration and Analysis for Science"

**Argonne Base**:
The DOE ASCR-funded umbrella program containing two co-equal thrusts, TIDES and IDEAS. Mentioned on the site only as context.
_Avoid_: the grant, the proposal

**Thrust**:
One of the two co-equal research efforts within Argonne Base.
_Avoid_: subproject, track

**TIDES**:
Transformative Integration of Data Enabling Science — the data-management thrust (Thrust 1) of Argonne Base. Out of scope for this site except as a sibling-thrust reference.

### Research content

**Objective**:
One of the four IDEAS research directions: Efficient Data Representations, AI-Driven Rendering and Visualization, Human-Centered Exploration, System Integration and Testing.
_Avoid_: aim, goal, pillar

**Use Case**:
A DOE science application that motivates and validates IDEAS research. Active: Cosmology (HACC) and Turbulence in Complex Geometries (NekRS). Upcoming: X-Ray Science (XPCS at APS).
_Avoid_: application domain, driver, Fusion Flow

**Homomorphic Representation**:
A compact, differentiable model of scientific data (e.g. INR, MFA, KAN) that preserves analytical and visual fidelity so rendering and analysis can operate on it directly.
_Avoid_: compressed data, neural compression

### People and events

**Partner**:
An external institution contributing to IDEAS, funded or not (UC Davis, UNL, UIC, Kitware, NVIDIA).
_Avoid_: collaborator site, subcontract, advisor

**Team Member**:
A named person on IDEAS, whether at Argonne or a Partner; all are presented as one team, not grouped by institution type.
_Avoid_: participant, personnel, staff

**Software Release**:
A publicly available tool produced or substantially extended by IDEAS (e.g. MFA, SASAV, NL2SciVis, GSCache, Vitral).
_Avoid_: product, artifact, tool

**News Item**:
A short dated announcement of something that has happened (a paper published, a software release). Kept separate from Events.
_Avoid_: post, blog entry, update

**Event**:
A dated gathering with a place and format that people attend (an All-Hands Meeting, workshop, or tutorial). Kept separate from News Items.
_Avoid_: meeting (when the specific type is known), news

**All-Hands Meeting**:
An in-person (or hybrid) gathering of the whole IDEAS team, including Partners. IDEAS-only — not the joint Argonne Base meeting.
_Avoid_: team meeting, kickoff, workshop
