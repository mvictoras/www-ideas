Short dated announcements of what has happened in IDEAS

Short dated announcements of what has happened in IDEAS — papers published and Software Releases. Newest first. Upcoming gatherings (All-Hands Meetings, workshops) are listed under Events, never here.

Sep 15, 2026 The IDEAS Software Releases are now publicly available open source — MFA, SASAV, NL2SciVis, GSCache and Vitral — with descriptions and repositories listed on the Software page.
Jun 08, 2026 NL2SciVis: A Benchmark for Natural Language to Scientific Visualization by Manish Mathai, Mengjiao Han, Janet Knowles, Victor A. Mateevitsi, Silvio Rizzi and Hank Childs was presented at EuroVis 2026 in Nottingham, UK.
Feb 01, 2026 Make the Fastest Faster: Importance Mask Synthesis for Interactive Volume Visualization Using Reconstruction Neural Networks by Jianxin Sun, David Lenz, Hongfeng Yu and Tom Peterka appeared in IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, vol. 32, no. 2.
Jan 01, 2026 GSCache: Real-Time Radiance Caching for Volume Path Tracing Using 3D Gaussian Splatting by David Bauer, Qi Wu, Hamid Gadirov and Kwan-Liu Ma appeared in IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, vol. 32, no. 1.
Nov 21, 2025 Our paper Do Large Language Models Speak Scientific Workflows? by Orcun Yildiz and Tom Peterka was presented at the Workshop on Workflows in Support of Large-Scale Science (WORKS) at SC’25.
