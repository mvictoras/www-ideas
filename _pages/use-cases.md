<p class="use-cases-lede">IDEAS exists for the scientists who use it. Two Use Cases drive the research today — Cosmology, where the data is measured in petabytes, and Turbulence in Complex Geometries, where it hides inside unforgiving reactor geometry — and a third, X-Ray Science at the Advanced Photon Source, is upcoming. Each stresses a different edge of the problem: sheer volume, geometric and multiphysics complexity, and the speed at which data arrive.</p>

<!-- use-cases -->

The active Use Cases double as validation grounds. The downscaled Frontier-E volume and the Taylor-Green Vortex are reproducible testbeds where the assistant is checked against data the whole community can regenerate, and the streaming patterns proven with them are the blueprint for the X-Ray Science integration. The [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page describes the Objectives that serve these Use Cases; the [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and [Software]({{ '/software/' | relative_url }}) pages track what has resulted so far.
